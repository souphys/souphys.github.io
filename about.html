About Me

Theoretical & Computational Solar Plasma Physicist

Biography

I am a researcher in theoretical and computational solar plasma physics at the Department of Physics, Tezpur University, India. My research focuses on solar plasma dynamics, helioseismology, magnetohydrodynamics (MHD), wave propagation, and energy transport in the solar atmosphere through analytical modelling and numerical simulations.
