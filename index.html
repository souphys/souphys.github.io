Welcome to my academic website. I am a researcher in theoretical and computational solar plasma physics. My research focuses on the theoretical modeling and numerical analysis of solar plasma dynamics, helioseismology, magnetohydrodynamics (MHD), wave propagation, and energy transport processes in the solar atmosphere.
